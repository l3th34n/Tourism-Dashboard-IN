About the Dashboard

The India Tourism Dashboard provides an interactive platform for exploring tourism statistics and trends across the country.

The platform aims to make tourism information easier to understand through statistics, charts and visual representations of official tourism data.

Data can be integrated from official sources including the Ministry of Tourism's tourism data portal.
